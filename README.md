# Sales Analysis Dashboard

**Author:** Amina Dahiru
**Project Type:** Data Analytics and Business Intelligence
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI

## Project Overview

The Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a visual analysis of sales performance, profitability, customer activity, purchasing patterns, revenue trends, and brand performance.

The objective is to transform sales data into actionable insights that support business decision-making, identify profitable customer segments, and improve overall business performance.

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales and Profitability

The dashboard reports total sales of 3M and total profit of 932K, providing an overview of the business's financial performance.

### 2. Profit by Income Level

The medium-income segment generates the highest displayed profit at 0.31M. High-income and low-income segments each contribute approximately 0.28M.

### 3. Purchase Distribution by Color

The purchasing distribution shows three groups representing approximately 39.09%, 33.4%, and 27.51% of purchases. The mapping between the displayed values and color categories should be verified before making inventory decisions.

### 4. Monthly Sales Performance

Sales remain relatively stable from January to March, increasing from approximately 0.34M in January to 0.35M in March.

### 5. Revenue Trend

Revenue is displayed at 1,035K in January 2023, 1,025K in February, and 1,047K in March, showing a recovery after the February decline.

### 6. Profit by Brand

Apple records the highest displayed brand profit at 197K, followed by Lenovo and Samsung at 160K each. Dell records the lowest displayed profit at 126K.

### 7. Regional Analysis

The dashboard provides filters for East, South, North, and West. Further analysis is needed to compare performance across these regions.

## Business Recommendations

* Investigate the factors contributing to Apple's higher profitability.
* Develop targeted strategies for the medium-income customer segment.
* Review pricing, costs, and margins for lower-profit brands.
* Investigate the factors behind March's improved sales performance.
* Improve data completeness for customer income classifications.
* Compare sales and profitability across all four regions.
* Monitor revenue, costs, and profit together to support sustainable growth.

## Project Objective

To demonstrate how data visualization and business intelligence can help stakeholders understand sales performance, identify business opportunities, and make informed decisions.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of an interactive Power BI dashboard to summarize sales and profitability metrics, analyze customer segments, compare brand performance, and identify monthly revenue trends.

The findings provide a foundation for further analysis and data-driven business improvement.
